About the company

What MusharaqaHub does

MusharaqaHub PLC is an IT company registered in the Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC). We develop specialised software for processing data about intellectual-property objects and producing structured digital documentation from it.

Important. The platform provides software tools for working with information and documents only. MusharaqaHub is not a financial institution, does not carry out transactions with money, securities or digital assets, does not issue or enable the circulation of any digital assets, and does not provide investment, brokerage, insurance or capital-placement advisory services.

Capabilities

Platform modules

Three functional modules covering the full cycle of digital data processing for an intellectual-property object.

MODULE 01

Digital processing of IP data

The full data-processing cycle for an IP object: analysis of submitted documents, structuring of information, preparation of legal and technical documentation, and a description of the object in a single digital format.

MODULE 02

Digital register of IP records

Maintenance of a structured digital register: recording IP objects, systematising information about their legal status and keeping the data up to date in a single information environment. A recording and document-management tool.

MODULE 03

Ethical-compliance check

A built-in module that analyses structured data and documentation for compliance with ethical-business principles, including Shariah norms, and produces an informational compliance report.

How it works

10 stages of digital IP-data processing

Work with an intellectual-property object goes through 10 sequential stages, each delivered by the platform's software tools.

1

Identifying IP objects

Analysis of the enterprise documents submitted by the user to identify objects suitable for digital processing.

2

Structuring and preparation

Preparation of the set of legal and accounting documents needed to record IP objects in the platform's digital register.

3

Documents for valuation

Preparation of the package of documents required to determine the initial value and substantiate the value of the IP object.

4

Personal-account setup

Configuration of a single user interface for managing data about IP objects.

5

Integration with external systems

Connecting the platform to the user's information systems via agreed data-exchange protocols.

6

Producing electronic documentation

Preparation, by platform tools, of electronic documents — reports, statements and descriptions of the IP object.

7

Connecting payment for services

Integration of payment providers to accept payment for platform access by bank card.

8

Notifications and services setup

Configuration of user notifications and auxiliary service functions of the platform.

9

Recording register changes

Technical configuration of the module for recording and updating information about IP objects in the digital register.

10

Technical maintenance

Ongoing support for the platform and integrated systems throughout the licence term.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is MusharaqaHub?

It is cloud software (SaaS) provided under a limited licence. It gives you tools to structure, record and document data about intellectual-property objects. It is a software product, not a financial service.

Is this a financial or investment product?

No. The platform does not carry out transactions with money, securities or digital assets, does not issue or circulate digital assets, and does not provide investment or brokerage services. All decisions a user makes based on the data are their own.

How is payment made?

By bank card (Visa, Mastercard) through internet acquiring. Card payments are processed in tenge (KZT) at the National Bank of Kazakhstan rate on the payment date. Payment can be made in tranches by agreement.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, under the conditions set out in our Refund Terms — in full before access is granted, and pro rata for work already performed thereafter. Refunds are made to the same bank card.

How is my data protected?

Data is transmitted over a secure HTTPS connection with access control and a protected personal account. Bank-card data is handled by the payment service and is not stored by us.

Who is the service for?

Individuals, sole proprietors and companies that need to structure and document data about their intellectual-property objects.
